## What I take from it

- **Head vs backbone.** On seed 1234, swapping v6's Y head for the inject head (same backbone) raises MI, EN and SSIM_vi and lowers SSIM_ir and MS-SSIM_ir. It also stops v6's global darkening and its brightening outside IR-brighter areas. Swapping the baseline backbone for FusionEdge's (same head) gives a 4–4 split on the holdout metrics, at 1.7× the parameters and 2.8× the latency.
- **The split follows the gate.** Baseline + inject opens its gate wider than FusionEdge (see the gate row above), so it adds more IR. That raises the IR-similarity scores and lowers the visible-similarity ones. The two models sit at different points on one trade-off rather than one being better overall.
- **Qualitative** (`docs/assets/showcase_holdout.jpg`, `docs/assets/gate_holdout.jpg`):
  - colour is preserved by all three models;
  - hot objects such as the truck wheels become near-white discs, with FusionEdge keeping slightly more rim detail;
  - at night the inject heads also add IR sensor grain on road surfaces;
  - v6 changes brightness well outside the thermal targets.
- **Seed repeats:** under the pre-registered rule, baseline + inject matches or beats FusionEdge on 5/8 holdout metrics, so the FusionEdge backbone is **not supported** as an improvement. This is a narrow verdict: the threshold is 5, and two of those metrics (EN, AG) are "no clear difference". FusionEdge is clearly better on the visible-similarity side (SSIM_vi, MS-SSIM_vi, SF), and baseline + inject on the IR side (MI, SSIM_ir, MS-SSIM_ir). FusionEdge also varies more across seeds: its gate settles at different openings, and its metric SDs are several times larger.

## What I would use

**`baseline_simplecnn_inject`**, the baseline backbone with the inject head (checkpoint `experiments/baseline_simplecnn_inject/checkpoints/last.pt`). Under the pre-registered rule it matches or beats FusionEdge on 5 of 8 holdout metrics. It is stable across seeds, 2.8× faster, and has 1.7× fewer parameters. FusionEdge stays in the repo as a negative result: its backbone did not hold up as an improvement.
